# Jack's quest line: the owner's own words

This is the full quest line with letters and dialogue, **exactly as Jack wrote it** (spelling and all). It is the basis for the quests and their triggers. Nothing here is in the game yet. When a quest is built, each spoken line or letter is copied word for word into the game and added to `JACKS_LINES.txt`, so it shows as "Jack's" in `DIALOGUE.md`. Do not tidy, shorten or reword anything without being asked.

---

Quest 1: A Stranger’s Letter, Part 1
You receive a letter, tied to the foot of a seagull, from a stranger on a mysterious island asking for help.
(Letter) 
“Please come quick! Strangers are taking over the northern water temple. There is no time to waste! Hurry” 
After travelling to the island, you find the stranger under attack from pirate bandits. You defeat the pirates one by one until you defeat them all. Before fleeing, one of the pirates tells you this isn’t over: when the rest of the fleet arrives, they will conquer all of the land and sea.
(Pirate Grunt)
“This isn't over! You'll see! Just wait till the rest of us get here! Then youl wish you had never messed with The Fleet!
You find the stranger hiding. When he comes out, he is so grateful that he gives you his grandfather’s compass. (This will let you navigate more easily.)
Thank you so much. They were just about to break into the sacred Vault. Thankfully you got my letter and made it here in time. Take this compass as a thanks. It will help show you the way when the seas get rough”
The old man walks off screen.
Reward: Grandfather’s compass.
After the quest: When you return to the village a villager runs up to you distressed
(Villager.)
“Have you heard! Pirates have been sited taking of fire island and setting up some kind of camp! Id steer clear of those waters if i was you. They look mean alright. You should go talk to Bjorn. I think i is looking for you.”
The villager then runs away. 
you find out that more pirate ships have been seen, and that they have set up a base on one of the islands. 
You are too weak at this stage to defeat these pirates. You will need to be a higher level and have better gear.

Quest 2: Find Rue
The village elder named “Bjorn the Elder” tells you they sent someone from the village to the mainland settlement to bring back weapons, so the village can defend itself, but he hasn’t returned. Can you find him and bring everything back safely? They tell you to speak with the blacksmith in the walled settlement.
(Bjorn)
“Ahh! Thanks for coming to see me. Im guessing you heard. Well look, I need your help. I have sent Rue to the mainland to get weapons and supplies for the village. If those Pirates show up here we don't want to be defenseless. But she should have been back by now. I need to go speak with Garrick about this and see if you can find Rue. Hurry on now.”  
(Rue is new character)
When you get to the settlement, the blacksmith hasn’t seen the man, but he has heard a rumour that a storm blew a ship off course further up the coast. 
(Garrick)
“I haven't seen Rue today. But i heard someone speaking about a ship that went down a little north of the forest. Why don't you go have a look up north. Don’t go by ship, though a storm is blowing through that ways. If you go through the forest, you’ll have better luck. But be careful, foul beasties lurk in that wood. Take this and stay safe” 
He gives you an old sword.
Navigate and survive the forest, and you will find Rue in the dark forest by the wood cutters cottage.
(Rue)
“Boy am i glad to see you, Bjorn sent the right person for the job lets get out of here eh”
When you get back to the blacksmith, he is impressed. He tells you he needs better metal to make better weapons, and he gives you the sword and a pickaxe. There are deposits all over the place, he says, so bring metal back to him and he will buy it from you.
Garrick. 
“Impressive work lad. Listen, you seem to be able to handle yourself pretty well. I need materials for my work. iron , copper, tim and such. How about we make a deal. You take this pick axe here and if you collect materials bring them back to me. I'll buy them from you for a good price.” 
(gives you pick axe and unlocks buying of pick axe in his shop)
When you return the man to the village, he tells you he is forever grateful. If you ever need someone to crew a ship, come and find him, he lives in the village. He says he is going to deliver the weapons around town, and that you should go and talk with Bjorn.
Rue
“I sure am grateful for you showing up. Now if you be looking for Crew for a ship one day you come fine me see. Im good on the ropes and fast in a fight. I'm gonna go deliver these weapons around now but you go speak to Bjorn. Be sure to tell him im grateful for him sending you”
When you speak with the elder, he gives you a healing potion and a stamina potion as a reward. He tells you to visit the village apothecary if you need more. He then says he is happy to have someone so capable: 
Bjorn the Elder
“Well well well. I knew I put my hopes in good hands. It's nice to know we have someone here we can rely on. Listen, take these. Helpful in a tight spot they are. If you're looking to make some extra coins I overheard Murl at the ship yard needing some help with something. Why don't you go and have a chat with him.
Reward: Pickaxe, sword, crew member (plus a healing potion and a stamina potion from the elder).

Quest 3: Merl’s Quest
When you go to find Merl, he is working on a ship by his workshop, carving the wood. He tells you he needs some special timber to build this ship’s mast. It needs to be extra hard, and the best timber for it grows on one of the islands. If you can find some and bring it back, he will make it worth your while.
(Murl)
“Old Bjorn sent ya did he? Well i need some help finding some timber. Im building a Mast for my next ship. I need some special wood for this though. Only the straightest strongest wood will do. Go to Oak Island Look for a hard wood mast tree. Bring me back one of those and ill make it worth your while”
When you finish, he gives you gold. He also tells you he has written to the woodcutter, who has a spare axe he isn’t using any more. You can have it if you go and get it.
(Murl)
“Well done! Here take this gold. But I wrote to my old friend Hale. He's that fella who cutts the timber in the darkwood. He says he had a spare axe you can have. You just gotta go get it and its all yours”
Reward: Gold 100; unlocks the axe from Hale.

Quest 4: A Stranger’s Letter, Part 2
When you next visit the mainland*, a seagull lands at your feet with a letter from the stranger from the start of the game. He has found out that the pirates are planning to attack and loot the Earth Temple in the northern mountains. He has sent for help, but you need to hurry!
Letter
“Iv just received word that the pirates are moving to the earth temple. Iv sent for help but you are closest at hand. If you hurry you can hopefully head them off. I think I have discovered there plans and they are dire indeed. You must stop them.”
You travel to the temple. After fighting enemies to gain entrance, you go in and overhear a grunt saying, 
(Pirate Grunt)
“You sure the boss is right? I can’t see no key?” 
(Pirate Grunt 2)
“Just quit your jabbering and keep looking!” the other replies.
You run in and defeat the two pirates. As they run away, one of them says, 
(Pirate Grunt 2)
“Fine, I guess it’s dynamite then, isn’t it!”
When they are gone, a woman magically appears. 
(Mysterious Young Girl)
“Congatrualations and thanks you.” 
She gives you a golden Key

(Mysterious Young Girl)
“it unlocks the chamber, along with the other keys from the Air, Water, Earth and Fire temples. It sounds like the pirates already have the Fire key, which means they will probably try to get the others. They have to be stopped. Collect all four keys and take them to our friend at the temple tower, where they will be safe.” 
Return this key to the temple tower. As you enter you find the mysterious old man from the water temple waiting for you.
(Mysterious old man)
“Oh Good work! I will keep this safe with the water temple key. I presume you might want to know what's going on here. Well I figured out that the pirate plans to use the keys to raise the seas. They want to drown the world. This is an ancient power. Each temple represents both life and destruction. Each Key opens its opposite. See that if why they attacked the water temple first. But we stopped them just in time. I think they have figured this out though and will no doubt be heading back to the water temple and finish what they started 
By collecting all four keys and inserting all four keys into the water temple they would flood this world and rule the ocean as kings. We cant let this happen and i dont think it is by accident that we have met. You, I believe, can stop these pirates and save your home. Take these boots. They will make you swift on your feet. Go back to the sea temple and see if you can get the key from the chamber inside. Then bring it back to me and here I will keep them safe.” 
There you find out that each key only unlocks its opposite, and that the pirates will be heading to the Sea temple next. As a reward, he gives you some enchanted boots for protection. He tells you to head to the Sea temple.
Reward: Enchanted boots letting you run faster

Quest 5: Saviour of the Seas

When you return the key to the tower, the woman from the Mountain temple walks into the tower temple. 
(Mysterious Girl)
I'm glad I caught you. I've brought you something to help in your quest! Take this with you.”
Reward: Enchanted armour.

Quest 6: At the Top of the World
On the way up the hill to the Air temple, you must once again fight your way to the top of the mountain. Halfway up, you hear an explosion.
You continue along the path. Just short of the top, pirates coming down the path intercept you. One of them is the big boss. He is glad in golden Armour with a black cape. He is tall and larger than any man you have ever seen.
(Caption Barron)
“Who is this little dog that blocks my way! Prepare yourself for your end!” 
 He sends his grunts to fight you, and when you beat them, he seems rattled. He kicks dirt in your eyes, and when you recover, they are gone.
You hear sobbing coming from the temple on top of the hill. You investigate and find a young boy crying at the destruction of the temple. 
(Mysterious your boy)
“Did the old man send you? I tried to stop them. But they blew it up. They have taken my key. Please tell him I'm sorry I couldn't stop them. Here take this. If it's true that the old man said and you are helping us then I want you to have this. Please stop those awful pirates and get my key back.”

Reward: Sky Helm (a helmet that gives an attacking speed bonus).

Quest 7: Under Attack
As you’re about to leave the island, a seagull arrives with a note: the village is under attack and calling for help.
(Letter From Bjorn the eldar)
“Pirates everywhere! Village under attack! We need all hands on deck for this fight. COME QUICK!”
When you reach the village, it is surrounded by pirate ships. Some are firing cannons into the village, houses are on fire, and villagers are fighting pirates. You fight off the pirates and they flee, but you learn that something else is going on. As they flee, you overhear something like, 
(Pirate Grunt)
“Let’s get out of here! The boss will be finished at the tower! Meet up back at the base and we will finish this!”
The pirates disappear, and you have completed the quest.

Quest 8: Into the Depths
When you return to the tower temple, you find it has also been attacked, and the pirates have taken the keys. A secret door has opened on the floor of the chamber. A letter tied to a seagull’s foot, from the old man, tells you to find him in the chamber below.
(Letter)
“Meet us in the chamber”
You enter the chamber, where monsters and ghosts guard the path. Once you defeat them, you enter the chamber and find the old man, the young boy from the Air temple, and the young girl from the Earth temple. They welcome you and tell you they are the spirits of these temples, and that they believe you can save the world. 
(Mysterious Old Man)
“I knew you would make it here. Those pirates might be strong but there is much they dont understand. But we need to tell you who we so you understand. We have not told you who we are. Because we are not people as you know. I am the spirit of the sea. And my brother and sister here are the spirits of the Earth and the Air. our sister of fire is still trying her best to protect the fire temple. But I fear she won't hold much longer against the forces trying to destroy this world. Unfortunately we underestimated those forces and they have taken out keys. We have only one path left. To ask you to help us to save this world. We didn't choose you by accident. We have been watching you since the night of your birth. But I always hoped I was wrong about you and you would simply live a life in peace. I was not wrong though. I have known for a long time that you are here to save this land. Not just a human but the spirit of Void. The fifth element is little understood but I knew you were representative the day you were born. Your mother Knew this so we have watched over you for all these years. Now the time has come that your destiny will unfold. Go to the fire temple. Save our sister… your sister. And us. Your brothers and sisters… Take this. This sword will aid you and help you to take your place as our defender and the defender of the world. Go now. You know what to do. We know you will not fail.”
For completing this quest, they give you an enchanted sword to fight the pirates.
Reward: Divine Blade.

Quest 9: The Final Fight
You travel to the Sea temple in a storm. You work through the pirates, defeating them one by one, and finally you meet the boss Capitan Barron
(Captain Barron)
“You! The dog! Every step my warriors have been talking about some little beast that won't stop hounding us. Well this is it for you. We won't be having a third battle. Prepare yourself this time you will die. Let's go!”
 defeat the boss.
(Captain Barron)
“I was wrong! You're greater than I… I don't know how this is possible.. Unless… Of course. I was a food you are the fifth aren't you. I should have read the legends more closely. It seems so obvious now. Let my Lads go will you. I give you my word that we won't come back.”
Once he is defeated, the sky clears and the last pirates flee, never to return.
The old man arrives, 
(mysterious old man/ Spirit of water)

You have done well and have earned your place among us. I'm sure I'll be seeing you again some time soon. Thank you.”

Travelling from temple to temple, you return the keys to each spirit.
(Not in order)
(Mysterious Young Boy/ Earth spirit)

“So it is true. Our long lost brother has returned. Thank you. Water has raised an island up from the sea just for you, so that you might find peace and a home to build your new life. Inside the letter is a key to a home on your new island. Keep this safe. You have done a great service to everyone on this land. Though they might not know it. This world owes you a great debt. Go well brother.”
The fire spirit gives you the letter and The island now appears on your map, the key enclosed gives you access to your own home and you have completed the main story of the game.

## Decisions Jack made about this text (11 Oct)

These are notes for the builders. The text above is untouched.

- **Rue is a woman.** She is the new character from Quest 2 (the lost villager who later joins your crew). Where the text says "the man" or "he" about Rue, the game will use "she" and "her" only when Jack rewrites those lines; until then the text stays as written. The old trader called Rue is now Murl (the boatyard), so there is no clash.
- **Murl** is the boatyard owner's name everywhere, including Quest 3's title.
- **Keys and temples:** the "Air" key belongs to the **Sky** temple and the "Water" key to the **Sea** temple. The other two are Earth and Fire.
- **Quest 1** happens on the **Sea temple island** (the "northern water temple").
